MEDISURE PREMIUM PLUS - 2024 POLICY SUMMARY

SECTION 4: PHARMACEUTICAL COVERAGE (FORMULARY)
- TIER 1 (Generic): $10 Co-pay. Includes Amoxicillin, Lisinopril, Metformin.
- TIER 2 (Preferred Brand): $40 Co-pay. Includes Lipitor, Nexium.
- TIER 3 (Non-Preferred): $100 Co-pay.

SECTION 7: SURGICAL COVERAGE
- Standard procedures: 90% covered.
- Advanced Knee Replacement: Covered ONLY if patient has failed 6 weeks of Physical Therapy first.
- Prior Authorization: Required for all elective surgeries.
